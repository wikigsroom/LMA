**What this demands of an implementation:**

The application must execute a user-selected SUPRA-Tex recipe entirely on the phone: image/alpha preprocessing, actual Qwen PBR material prediction, aligned geometry and partial observations, trained static-observation interleaved completion, four sparse VAE networks, and a textured PBR GLB. CPU replacements for CUDA kernels may change implementation while retaining tensor, coordinate, material, and sampling semantics. Neither a generic image generator, sparse operator library, precomputed latent import, nor a catalog entry establishes this property. The source completion boundary takes local geometry/camera/RGBA/albedo/MRA, while its frontend supplies the preceding image-to-geometry/material stages (`UP/supratex/inference/pipeline.py:L181-L219`; `UP/supratex/frontend/pipeline.py:L164-L215`).

The minimal complete route uses **one locally observed front view**. Upstream already permits single-view completion with no generated back view (`UP/supratex/inference/pipeline.py:L654-L677`). Its external dual-view workflow must not be copied into P05: an outside back-view image is unnecessary for that valid single-view route and prohibited by the quoted requirement.
