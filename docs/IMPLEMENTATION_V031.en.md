See the frozen [base implementation](IMPLEMENTATION_V030.en.md) for their detailed routes; current source identities live in the v0.3.1 matrix and verification records.

The additional recipes are program-integrated and their source layouts were checked. This is not trained-model execution or phone acceptance. Newer video architectures and video LoRA remain explicitly outside these registered routes; concrete metadata research is in the community guide. No prior selected target is deferred or removed.
