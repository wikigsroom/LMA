# LMA v0.3.1 — 社区模型与内容标记 / Community models and content labels

本次在完整本地多模态基座上新增可选社区权重目录、独立内容标记和来源复核；所有模型仍由用户选择、下载和管理，APK 不附权重。This update extends the existing local foundation with optional community checkpoints, independent content provenance and source review. Models remain user-controlled.

模型来源包括 Realistic Vision、RealVisXL、Juggernaut、Animagine、Kohaku、OrangeMix、Pony 镜像及 NSFW Wan 1.3B；其他没有足够内容声明的条目如实标为未标注。Pony 的镜像身份、HF 许可字段与原作者修改版条款冲突均明确显示，不能视为商用授权。

The 14 video variants are EXP E01–E14 from one Wan 1.3B series. The publisher suggests E14; no quality claim is made. Source-reported legacy checkpoint problems remain visible in the guide. Every model is a peer with independent output-task, foundation/dependency, content and installation labels.
